Question 1: Why is a stack the right choice for undo/redo?
A stack is a good choice for an undo and redo system because it follows the Last In, First Out system. 
This means the last action that was added will be the first one removed. This makes sense because when someone presses undo, 
they usually want to undo the most recent thing they did. When an action is undone, it gets moved to the redo stack in case the
user wants to bring that action back. Having two stacks helps keep everything in the correct order.

Question 2: Why is a queue better suited for the help desk?
A queue works better for a help desk because it follows the First In, First Out system. This means whoever enters the queue 
first should also be helped first. This is important for a help desk because it keeps the system fair and organized instead of
helping customers in a random order. The front keeps track of who should be helped next, while the rear keeps track of where a 
new customer should be added.
